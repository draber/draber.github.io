# 📦 Spelling Bee Assistant 6.0.0

**Release Date:** to be announced

## New

- **Pin the Grid** (desktop): click the 📌 in the Grid pop-up and it stays open next to the game, updates with every word you find and can be moved anywhere by its title bar. Position and pinned state are remembered.

## Improvements

- Pop-ups no longer depend on the NYT’s own pop-up system, which makes them far less likely to break when the NYT changes the Spelling Bee page.
- The Assistant menu can no longer be covered by the Assistant panel.
- On small screens with many open panes, the Assistant panel scrolls instead of sliding under the header.
- Escape and a click outside close pop-ups everywhere, also after reopening them several times.

## Bug Fixes

- Keyboard shortcuts with Ctrl are shown as “Ctrl” in the shortcut list.
- Pangrams are highlighted reliably in the word list.
- The “new feature” marker in the menu disappears as intended after a few days.
